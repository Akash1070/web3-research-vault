## 👋 What Is This Repository? (The Simple Version)

Hey! If you have ever tried learning about Web3, crypto, or how blockchains actually work behind the scenes, you probably noticed two really frustrating things:
1. **Shady 'gurus' and fake course sellers** trying to charge hundreds of dollars for basic slides and get-rich-quick hype.
2. **Real academic research papers** being locked behind expensive university paywalls or scattered across obscure forums where nobody can find them.

We got tired of that. So we built this vault.

This is an **open-source, 100% free digital library containing 368 real research papers, protocol whitepapers, and market reports** (~1.86 GB of pure knowledge). You will find everything from Satoshi Nakamoto's original 2008 Bitcoin paper to the math behind Uniswap's automated market maker, zero-knowledge proofs, and Ethereum rollup designs.

You do not need to pay a single dollar. You do not need to sign up for anything. Just clone the repo or read the papers right in your browser on our free web reader at **[YourWeb3Guy.com/learn](https://www.yourweb3guy.com/learn)**.

### What is the Web3 Research Vault?
The **Web3 Research Vault** is a centralized, free, open-access public library founded by **Akash Kumar Jha ([YourWeb3Guy](https://www.yourweb3guy.com))**. It archives 368 verified PDF manuscripts covering blockchain cryptography, consensus algorithms, DeFi protocols, smart contract security audits, and token economics.

### Who is this for?
- **Students & Self-Learners:** High school students, college undergrads, and developers who want to understand the real computer science behind blockchain without hype.
- **Smart Contract Engineers:** Developers looking to learn math invariants, attack vectors, and protocol designs.
- **Researchers & Analysts:** Crypto researchers who need quick offline access to foundational papers from IEEE, MDPI, arXiv, and leading blockchain foundations.

Yes! Go to **[https://www.yourweb3guy.com/learn](https://www.yourweb3guy.com/learn)**. Every single document in this vault is hosted on an interactive dark-mode reader with search filters, key bullet takeaways, and read-time estimates.
